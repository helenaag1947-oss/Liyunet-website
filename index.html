ABOUT LIYUNET

A transport partner built around reliability.

Liyunet Transport and Logistics P.L.C. is based in Addis Ababa, near Kality Main Custom Office, and has experience in transport and logistics services in Ethiopia for more than 10 years.

We are developing modern, digitalized transport and logistics services focused on efficiency, quality, safety, security and flexibility. Our qualified management and staff work to understand customer requirements and provide dependable cargo movement.
